## Featured Engineering Projects

### HeyCileng AgTech Platform
*B2B Circular Agriculture Waste Marketplace*

HeyCileng AgTech connects Nigerian agro-processors and commercial off-takers to turn agricultural waste (such as cassava peels, crop stalks, sugarcane bagasse, and rice husks) into valuable industrial inputs. Built to reduce post-harvest waste across Nigerian supply chains.

* Platform Features: Paystack inline payment escrow, weighing bridge log verification, Supabase Edge Functions with Brevo SMTP, PostGIS spatial indexing, and PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS).
* Identity Verification: Integrated live identity verification supporting NIN and CAC registration via Odot Services API.
* Stack: JavaScript, React, Supabase, PostgreSQL, PostGIS, Edge Functions, Paystack API, HTML5/CSS3.

### Decentralized IP & Patent Research Marketplace
*Web3 Academic Research & Asset Management Platform*

A decentralized platform for registering, trading, and licensing intellectual property, patents, and scientific research datasets.

* Engineering Highlights: Solidity smart contracts deployed on the Sepolia Ethereum testnet, decentralized media and metadata storage on IPFS, and Chainlink oracles for off-chain data validation.
* Academic Context: Developed as part of ongoing undergraduate software engineering research and thesis work.
* Stack: Solidity, Sepolia Ethereum Testnet, Chainlink, IPFS, Web3.js, React.

### Decentralized Certificate Verification System
*Immutable Credential Verification*

A smart contract system designed to eliminate academic credential fraud by issuing tamper-proof, verifiable digital certificates on-chain.

* Stack: Solidity, Web3.js, Ethereum, HTML/CSS.

### Adamawa State Smart City Architecture Proposal
*Regional Governance & Infrastructure Platform*

A Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) design proposal for smart city monitoring and governance in Adamawa State, combining IoT data ingest, distributed ledgers, and automated urban resource allocation.

* Stack: Software Architecture, Systems Design, IoT/Blockchain Systems.
